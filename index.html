中文

一、概述

《大鱼号》是一款以韩国历史与文化为主题的学习类应用，提供景点、韩食、韩语、韩流（K-Pop / 韩剧）与知识测验等内容，帮助读者轻松了解韩国文化。我们以「最小收集」为原则设计本应用，尊重并保护您的隐私。

二、我们收集的信息

本应用不要求注册账号，也不会在您正常使用过程中自动收集您的姓名、电话号码、精确地理位置、通讯录等个人身份信息。应用内置的景点、韩食、韩语、韩流、测验等全部内容均随应用本地存储，无需联网即可浏览，我们不会在此过程中收集您的任何数据。

三、网络请求与第三方公开接口

本应用以本地内容为主，但首页为丰富展示内容，会向以下公开接口发起请求以获取公开信息；这些请求不含任何个人身份信息，也不用于识别您的身份：

  • REST Countries：获取韩国国家概况（首都、人口、货币、语言等）。
  • Open-Meteo：获取多个城市的实时与 7 天天气（首尔、釜山、仁川、济州、大邱、大田等，使用应用内置的城市坐标，而非您的设备定位）。
  • Wikipedia：获取景点与美食的摘要及缩略图。

上述请求均为只读 GET 请求，失败时自动回退到应用内置的兜底内容，不会因此收集或上传任何个人信息。

四、反馈与联系

您可自愿通过应用内的「建议反馈」或「联系我们」功能，使用您设备上的邮件应用向我们发送邮件。我们仅在您主动发送时收到您填写的称呼（可选）与反馈内容，该过程通过本机 mailto: 调用完成，不经由任何服务器上传。您发送的内容仅用于改进产品与服务。

五、评分引导

应用可能会在您打开时弹出好评引导；点击「去好评」会调用系统原生的 App Store 评价弹窗（由苹果控制展示频率），此过程不会向我们传输任何个人信息。

六、本地数据

应用使用 iOS 系统提供的 UserDefaults 在您的设备本机保存以下信息，不会上传至任何服务器：

  • 界面语言偏好（中文 / English）；
  • 应用安装日期、启动次数（用于评分引导逻辑）。

七、儿童隐私

本应用为文化学习类应用，适合各年龄段用户。我们特别重视儿童隐私，不会收集儿童的个人信息，也不会在应用中设置任何需要儿童提供个人信息的环节。若您是未成年人的监护人，发现我们意外收集了儿童信息，请通过下方邮箱联系我们，我们将及时删除。

八、数据安全

您在本应用中的偏好数据保存在设备本机。通过邮件发送的反馈由您使用的邮件服务提供商按其隐私政策进行处理。我们采取合理措施保护您主动提供的内容，但请注意任何电子邮件传输都存在一定风险。

九、您的权利

您有权了解我们如何处理您的信息，并可随时通过下方邮箱要求我们删除您主动发送的反馈内容。我们将在合理时间内予以响应。

English

1. Overview

Dayu Hao is an educational app themed around Korean history and culture, offering sights, food, language, Hallyu (K-Pop / K-Drama) and quizzes to help readers learn about Korean culture. We follow a minimal-collection principle and respect your privacy.

2. Information We Collect

The app does not require registration and does not automatically collect personal identity information such as your name, phone number, precise location, or contacts during normal use. All built-in content (sights, food, language, Hallyu, quizzes) is stored locally and can be browsed offline; we do not collect any data in the process.

3. Network Requests & Third-Party Public APIs

The app is primarily local, but to enrich content the home page fetches from the following public APIs to retrieve public information. These requests contain no personal data and are not used to identify you:

  • REST Countries: country profile of South Korea (capital, population, currency, languages, etc.).
  • Open-Meteo: current & 7-day weather for several cities (Seoul, Busan, Incheon, Jeju, Daegu, Daejeon), using the app's built-in city coordinates — not your device location.
  • Wikipedia: summaries and thumbnails for sights and food.

These are read-only GET requests that fall back to bundled content on failure; no personal information is collected or uploaded.

4. Feedback & Contact

You may voluntarily use the in-app feedback or contact feature to email us via your device's mail app. We only receive the name (optional) and feedback content you actively provide, sent through a local mailto: call — not uploaded to any server. Your message is used solely to improve the product.

5. Review Prompt

The app may show a review prompt when opened; tapping "Rate" invokes the system App Store review dialog (frequency controlled by Apple). This transmits no personal information to us.

6. Local Data

The app uses iOS UserDefaults to store the following on your device only, never uploaded to any server:

  • Interface language preference (Chinese / English);
  • App install date and launch count (used for the review-prompt logic).

7. Children's Privacy

This is an educational app suitable for all ages. We pay special attention to children's privacy and do not collect personal information from children, nor do we include any flow that asks children for personal information. If you are a guardian and believe we inadvertently collected a child's information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Data Security

Your in-app preference data stays on your device. Feedback sent by email is handled by your email provider per its policy. We take reasonable measures to protect the content you voluntarily provide, but please note that any email transmission carries some inherent risk.

9. Your Rights

You may inquire how your information is handled and request deletion of feedback you voluntarily sent, by contacting the email below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be published in-app and on the hosting page, with the "Effective Date" shown at the top.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or wish to exercise your rights, please contact us at 514389292@qq.com.
